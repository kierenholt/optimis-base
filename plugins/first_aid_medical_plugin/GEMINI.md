# Medical Room & First Aid Plugin (`plugins/first_aid_medical_plugin`)

## Purpose
Implements **Vertical 6** (`uk.org.optimis.medical.firstaid`) — the **Medical Room & First Aid Register** plugin for School Nurses, First Aiders, and Pastoral Support staff (`SUPPORT`, `DSL`, `HOD`, `ADMIN`). Allows staff to select an injured or unwell pupil, immediately view `CRITICAL` anaphylaxis/IHCP medical alerts and emergency response plans alongside verified safe emergency collection contacts (strictly excluding court-order-blocked guardians and redacting confidential refuge household addresses), and log treatments into `plugin_first_aid.db` while emitting the post-commit custom event `plugin.uk.org.optimis.medical.firstaid.logged`.

## Files
* [`manifest.json`](file:///home/kieren/Documents/code/optimis/plugins/first_aid_medical_plugin/manifest.json): Declares plugin ID `uk.org.optimis.medical.firstaid`, attached database alias `plugin_first_aid`, UK GDPR SAR hooks (`export_pupil_data`, `purge_pupil_data`), interactive RPC endpoints (`get_pupil_first_aid_profile`, `log_first_aid_treatment` requiring `dal.pupils.read:all`, `dal.medical.read:all`, `dal.guardians.read:all`), and background post-commit worker (`on_first_aid_logged` subscribed to `plugin.uk.org.optimis.medical.firstaid.logged`).
* [`migrations/001_init.sql`](file:///home/kieren/Documents/code/optimis/plugins/first_aid_medical_plugin/migrations/001_init.sql): Creates `first_aid_treatments` and `first_aid_event_logs` tables and pupil-indexed lookups inside `plugin_first_aid.db`.
* [`src/init.lua`](file:///home/kieren/Documents/code/optimis/plugins/first_aid_medical_plugin/src/init.lua):
  * Annotated with EmmyLua `@param ctx OptimisCtx` types from [`sdk/lua/ctx_types.lua`](file:///home/kieren/Documents/code/optimis/sdk/lua/ctx_types.lua).
  * `get_pupil_first_aid_profile(ctx, args)`: Queries `ctx.dal.pupils.list_all`, `ctx.dal.pupils.get_by_id`, `ctx.dal.pupils.list_sen_and_medical_batch` (`dal.medical.read:all`), and `ctx.dal.guardians.list_for_pupil` (`dal.guardians.read:all`), returning `CRITICAL` medical/IHCP conditions (including `medication_name_and_dose`, `medication_location`, `triggers`, `reaction_symptoms`, and `parental_consent_given`), court-order-filtered and refuge-redacted emergency contacts (`authorised_to_collect`, `contact_priority`), treatment history, and post-commit event dispatch logs.
  * `log_first_aid_treatment(ctx, args)`: Fail-fast validates mandatory treatment inputs, snapshots the pupil's active `CRITICAL`/IHCP alert (including medication dose and storage location) and primary safe guardian contact, inserts the treatment row into `plugin_first_aid.first_aid_treatments`, and buffers `ctx.events.emit("plugin.uk.org.optimis.medical.firstaid.logged", ...)` for post-commit dispatch.
  * `on_first_aid_logged(ctx, event)`: Post-commit background worker subscribed to `plugin.uk.org.optimis.medical.firstaid.logged` that persists an audit confirmation record (`DISPATCHED_POST_COMMIT`) in `plugin_first_aid.first_aid_event_logs`.
  * `export_pupil_data(ctx, args)` & `purge_pupil_data(ctx, args)`: Mandatory UK GDPR Subject Access Request (SAR) export and retention purge hooks for `plugin_first_aid.db`.
* [`ui/dist/index.html`](file:///home/kieren/Documents/code/optimis/plugins/first_aid_medical_plugin/ui/dist/index.html): Sandboxed micro-frontend (`<iframe sandbox="allow-scripts">`, `connect-src 'none'`) wired via `/sdk/mis-bridge.js` and declarative `data-mis-rpc` buttons (`get_pupil_first_aid_profile`, `log_first_aid_treatment`), rendering `CRITICAL` IHCP alerts with `medication_name_and_dose`, `medication_location`, `triggers`, and `PARENTAL_CONSENT_VERIFIED` badges.
